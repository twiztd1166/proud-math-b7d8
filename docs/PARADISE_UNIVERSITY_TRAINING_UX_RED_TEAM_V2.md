# Paradise University — Training Experience v2 Red-Team Control

Date: 2026-08-17

## Objective

Reduce cognitive load without deleting controlled curriculum or trainer-source material.

## Employee experience controls

1. Continue Training defaults to the Canvasser Core only: Foundation → Field Ready → Canvasser.
2. Senior, Sales Apprentice, Sales Rep, and Canvass Manager remain available under Career Path but do not silently enter the default queue.
3. Training Home has one dominant Continue Training action plus exactly four primary tools: Practice, Videos & Audio, Career Path, and My Progress.
4. The full career ladder and complete Canvassing Library are not repeated on Training Home.
5. Videos & Audio shows each media item at most once on the curated screen and explicitly prioritizes Tony Hoty New Canvasser Training — Process and Canvassing 101 before broader supporting media.
6. Trainer-specific browsing remains in Complete Canvassing Library / Source Library, not as duplicate playlists on Videos & Audio.
7. Sales Apprentice is a bridge, not a duplicate Sales Rep curriculum: What Changes and What Does Not → Full Sales Process Map → Sales Shadowing → Sales Apprentice Readiness.
8. Detailed Sales Rep method remains in Sales Rep Academy.
9. Required Quick Checks appear inside PASS before completion/Next and block forward navigation until passed.
10. Lessons with no media omit the empty WATCH / LISTEN block.
11. Sales Rep policy/control evidence remains available but is collapsed under Sales Controls & References after the lesson list.
12. The incorrect practice lineage pointer lesson:canvass-appointment is remapped to lesson:canvass-close.

## Non-changes

- No municipality dataset changes.
- No field-engine changes.
- No change to the exact canvass opener text.
- No trainer content deletion.
- No production promotion.

## Validation

Training Experience v2 must be enforced by static validation plus Playwright red-team regression tests before it becomes a candidate runtime.
